# Reference sources

Public-domain and U.S. government publications used for AerospaceBot equations, constants, and small physics programs. Entries were screened on 3 October 2026.

Works of U.S. federal employees, prepared as official duties, are not protected by copyright in the United States (17 U.S.C. § 105). NTRS records marked “Work of the US Gov. Public Use Permitted” are accepted when the item is an official NACA or NASA report, TM, SP, or RP, or when the authors are NASA, NACA, NOAA, FAA, or NIST employees. NASA Glenn educational pages that state they were developed in the public domain are included on that basis.

Mathematical relations are not copyrightable. The wording, figures, and tabulated data of a copyrighted work remain protected. Contractor and grantee documents on nasa.gov or NTRS often remain under copyright even when NASA may distribute them; those items are listed under Excluded sources.

## Structures

Motor-case hoop stress, margin of safety, and weld mismatch follow NASA SP-8025, *Solid Rocket Motor Metal Cases* (April 1970), as recorded with those formulas. Additional structural handbooks (Roark, Bruhn, and similar commercial texts) are not used.
