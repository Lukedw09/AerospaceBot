FAQ

Questions

Does this work in Cursor and in Grok Bot?

Yes. Both take the same MCP configuration from the install page. The client id is shared. You sign in with your own Google or Meta account.

Why Google or Meta?

That login is the credential for the plugin. Astraeus does not issue a separate password. You can link the other provider from Account after you are signed in.

What is a tool call?

One run of an aerospace tool: atmosphere, orbit, shock, propulsion, and the rest of the catalogue. Each run counts toward the daily cap on your plan. Account shows the count after you sign in.

How do I sign out?

Account has two actions. Sign out of this browser clears the session in this window. Sign out everywhere ends the other sessions too.

How do I delete my account?

Account asks you to confirm, then deletes the account and its pictures. That cannot be undone from this site.

Where does the plugin live?

The MCP endpoint is mcp.astraeus.de-wet.com/mcp. This site is astraeus.de-wet.com. The install snippet carries the endpoint and the client id.
